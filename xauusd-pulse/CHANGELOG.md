# Changelog

All notable changes to XAUUSD Pulse. Published by John Mugendi Kinyua.

## 2.0.0
- New neon-glass interface: animated gradient background, live headline ticker tape, glowing price with flash on every move, animated gauges, confetti on "good to go", dark and light themes, local fonts (works offline).
- Publisher credit (John Mugendi Kinyua) in the header, footer and page metadata.
- Realistic demo prices in sample mode; documentation PDF, README and screenshots; MIT licence; CI workflow.

## 1.5.0
- Loads up to 8 years of candle history in the background (paced for the free plan); backtests and the lab use all of it.
- Event pause around high-impact US events (live safety filter), position-size calculator, next-event countdown, optional alert sound, CSV exports.

## 1.4.0
- Four strategies (trend pullback v1 and v2, breakout, oversold bounce) compared side by side in the Test lab.
- On-site alerts (bell, banner), "good to go" only after two passes at least 12 hours apart, live signal journal (forward test).
- Fixed: sample data could trigger alerts and mix with real data.

## 1.3.0
- Test lab: data-quality check, results by period, walk-forward test, 243-setting sweep, higher-cost test, bootstrap luck check, seven-check verdict.

## 1.2.x
- Data check page, retry after a failed backtest, 5000-bar history, years in dates, permanent Settings panel for the API key.

## 1.1.0
- In-page API key box; sample and real candles are never mixed.

## 1.0.0
- Live price ticker, candles, RSI + EMA signal engine, backtester, news with gold and dollar impact, sentiment gauges, economic calendar.
